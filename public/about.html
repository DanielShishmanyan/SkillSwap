SkillSwap is a place to connect with people who want to teach and learn. Exchange skills, make a plan together, and meet for an online lesson.

Show up and learn.

Open your private session page to join Google Meet, suggest a different time, cancel, or confirm that the lesson is complete.

Google Calendar, when you choose.

Connecting Calendar is optional for using SkillSwap. To organize an automatic Google Meet lesson, connect the Google account that matches your SkillSwap email. With your permission, SkillSwap creates your lesson event and meeting link, invites both participants, and updates or cancels that event when your plan changes.

We request permission to manage events on calendars you own. Our integration uses it for SkillSwap lesson events; it does not browse unrelated calendar events. See the Google data section of our privacy policy for details.

Growing, one lesson at a time.

SkillSwap is an early-access service. Shared online lesson booking is available; some discovery, swap, and payment screens include demonstration data. Current payment screens simulate a payment and do not charge you. Calendar notifications depend on your settings; separate email reminders are not yet active.
