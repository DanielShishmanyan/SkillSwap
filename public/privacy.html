Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 20, 2026

SkillSwap uses account information to sign you in and lesson details to connect you with your partner. Google Calendar access is optional and is used for SkillSwap lesson events. We do not sell your personal data or use Google user data for advertising or AI model training.

1. Who operates SkillSwap

SkillSwap is operated by Daniel Shishmanyan, who is responsible for the personal data processed for this service. In this policy, “SkillSwap,” “we,” and “us” refer to this service and its operator. Contact: botevidani@gmail.com.

This policy covers the SkillSwap website and its account, skill-sharing, and online lesson features. Google and other providers also process information under their own privacy policies when you use their services.

2. Information we collect and why

  • Account and sign-in information: your name, email address, account identifier, verification status, and authentication credentials or tokens. Google sign-in provides identity information such as your name, email, and account identifier. If you use a password, it is sent securely to our authentication service; SkillSwap does not store a readable password in its application database. We use email verification codes to complete sign-in.
  • Profile and communication information: the skills, biography, availability, location, profile image, and messages you choose to enter. We use these details to support profiles, connections, and conversations. Some early-access features store this information only in your browser.
  • Lesson information: participant names, email addresses and account identifiers, lesson title, proposed and confirmed times, time zone, duration, notes, meeting and calendar links, changes, completion confirmations, and attendance reports. We use this information to manage your shared session and communicate the current plan.
  • Security and technical information: request details such as IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, delivery results, and error records may be processed by SkillSwap and its hosting providers. SkillSwap uses keyed digests of account or network identifiers for rate limiting and stores verification-code hashes rather than readable codes in its database. This helps prevent abuse and diagnose failures.

We process information needed to provide the service you request, such as creating an account or booking a lesson. We rely on your authorization for optional Google Calendar access, our legitimate interests for proportionate security and service maintenance, and legal obligations where they apply. We do not use this website to collect card details or process real payments in the current version.

3. Google sign-in and Google Calendar data

Google sign-in and connecting Google Calendar are separate actions. You can decline Calendar access and still use features that do not need it. Automatic Meet booking requires the organizer to connect a Google account matching their SkillSwap email.

The Calendar connection requests your verified email and the permission calendar.events.owned, which allows access to create, view, change, and delete events on calendars you own. SkillSwap uses this permission to create lesson events in your primary calendar, generate Google Meet links, invite both participants, retrieve those events when needed, and update or cancel SkillSwap lesson events. Our integration does not list or browse your unrelated calendar events.

We send Google the lesson title, schedule, time zone, notes, participant email addresses, and SkillSwap session link needed for the event. We receive and store the event identifier, calendar link, meeting link, and authorization information. Your partner and Google Calendar invitation recipients can see the event details you share. Avoid putting sensitive personal information in lesson titles or notes.

Refresh tokens are encrypted before storage and used to keep the Calendar connection working. We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models. Human access is limited to what is necessary for support with your permission, security and abuse investigations, legal requirements, or other access permitted by Google’s policy.

We follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke SkillSwap’s access through your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized Calendar operations; it does not automatically delete events already in Google Calendar or records already stored by SkillSwap. Contact us to request deletion of stored connection or lesson data.

4. Who receives information

  • Your lesson partner: shared lesson details, participant names and email addresses, meeting links, and attendance reports are available to both participants. Profile details you choose to share may be visible to other signed-in members.
  • Google/Firebase: authentication and database services; Google Calendar and Meet when you connect them. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Vercel: website hosting and server functions, including technical request information. See Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • Resend: recipient email addresses and message content necessary to send verification codes and lesson notifications. See Resend’s privacy policy.
  • Image providers: loading external images can send your IP address and browser request information to their providers. The current default-avatar service, UI Avatars, also receives the name used to generate the avatar.

We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate abuse, or protect legal rights. Provider infrastructure may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where required, applicable transfer safeguards must be used. We do not promise that all information stays in a single country.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Authentication and shared lesson records are stored using Firebase services. Some profile, conversation, and demonstration features use local browser storage. Authentication state may also be stored on your device, and a short-lived secure cookie is used during Calendar connection. Clearing browser data can remove locally stored details but does not delete your server account or shared lessons.

We use access controls, encrypted network connections, encrypted Calendar refresh credentials, and sign-in safeguards. No service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password and email codes private.

We retain account and lesson information while needed to operate the service, resolve issues, and meet applicable obligations. This early-access version does not yet have an automatic account or lesson deletion schedule. Verification codes expire after ten minutes, but expiration alone does not mean every related database or log record is immediately deleted. Provider backup and log retention can differ. You can ask us to delete account, Calendar connection, or lesson information; we may retain limited records where legally required or necessary for security or an unresolved dispute.

6. Your choices and rights

You can edit available profile fields, decline optional Calendar access, revoke Google access, clear local browser data, and request help with access, correction, export, restriction, objection, or deletion by contacting us. We may ask for reasonable proof that the request concerns your account. Withdrawing optional authorization does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We do not currently offer a self-service account deletion button; email us for an account or data deletion request. Events and copies already received by your partner or stored in Google Calendar may need to be deleted separately by their owners.

SkillSwap is not designed to collect sensitive personal information. Do not put passwords, financial details, health information, or confidential third-party data in your public profile or lesson notes.

7. Policy changes

We may update this policy as SkillSwap develops. The date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be explained through an appropriate notice, and additional authorization will be requested when required before using data for a new purpose.
